# User Research

> **Kodaro house context:** read [BRAND.md](../../BRAND.md) before starting. It holds Kodaro's and Jareeye's tokens, voice, quality bar and audiences.

Help plan, execute, and synthesize user research studies.

## Research Methods

| Method | Best For | Sample Size | Time |
|--------|----------|-------------|------|
| User interviews | Deep understanding of needs and motivations | 5-8 | 2-4 weeks |
| Usability testing | Evaluating a specific design or flow | 5-8 | 1-2 weeks |
| Surveys | Quantifying attitudes and preferences | 100+ | 1-2 weeks |
| Card sorting | Information architecture decisions | 15-30 | 1 week |
| Diary studies | Understanding behavior over time | 10-15 | 2-8 weeks |
| A/B testing | Comparing specific design choices | Statistical significance | 1-4 weeks |

## Interview Guide Structure

1. **Warm-up** (5 min): Build rapport, explain the session
2. **Context** (10 min): Understand their current workflow
3. **Deep dive** (20 min): Explore the specific topic
4. **Reaction** (10 min): Show concepts or prototypes
5. **Wrap-up** (5 min): Anything we missed? Thank them.

## Analysis Framework

- **Affinity mapping**: Group observations into themes
- **Impact/effort matrix**: Prioritize findings
- **Journey mapping**: Visualize the user experience over time
- **Jobs to be done**: Understand what users are hiring your product to do

## Kodaro Research Playbook

### Client discovery call (process stage *i. Discovery*, ~1 week)
Kodaro's first conversation is **free with no obligation**, and no technical brief is needed. Use it to learn:
1. What the business does and who its customers are
2. How the work runs **today** (paper, spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups?)
3. The one outcome that would make the project a success (enquiries, bookings, fewer errors)
4. Content they already have (logo, photos, copy) and gaps (design & branding upsell)
5. Deadline, budget comfort and who approves
6. Ownership expectations (domain, hosting, code). Explain "yours to keep".
Output: a one-page discovery summary that feeds the **written quote**.

### Local fieldwork (Jareeye, client sites)
- **Language:** run sessions in Somali by default. Offer English or Arabic. Record which was used.
- **Devices:** test on the participant's own phone where possible (Android, mid-range), on their real network.
- **Recruiting:** customers via WhatsApp groups and local businesses. Riders only if **approved**.
- **Sample size:** 5–6 per segment is enough to find most usability issues.
- **Consent:** verbal consent, explained plainly. Don't store phone numbers in notes.
- **Tasks for Jareeye:** find a nearby rider · call a rider · switch language · (rider) apply and go online.

### Operational users (AOMS)
- Observe a real shift workflow where permitted, but **never** capture real operational data. Use sample data.
- Focus on handovers, glanceability and error recovery.

## Deliverables

- Discovery summary (feeds the written quote)
- Research plan (objectives, methods, timeline, participants, language)
- Interview guide (questions, probes, activities)
- Synthesis report (themes, insights, recommendations)
- Highlight reel (key quotes and observations)
