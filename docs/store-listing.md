## Full description (max 4000)

### English

Jareeye is the fastest way to send something across Hargeisa.

Open the app, see the moto drivers who are online near you on a live map, and call one with a single tap. Meet the driver, hand over your package and the receiver's phone number, and the driver delivers it. You pay the driver directly.

WHY JAREEYE
• Live map of the motos online near you, nearest first
• One tap to call the driver; no waiting for an order to be accepted
• Every driver is checked and approved by the Jareeye team
• Each driver has a face photo and a Jareeye number (like JRY-007), so you know exactly who is coming
• Star ratings from other customers, so you can choose trusted drivers
• Works in English, Somali and Arabic
• Free to use; sign in with your phone number and a 4-digit PIN

FOR MOTO DRIVERS
Register in the app with your photo and bike plate. Once the Jareeye team approves you, go Online and customers nearby can see and call you. Busy with a delivery? Tap "I'm on a delivery" and you'll be hidden until you're free again.

Jareeye currently serves Hargeisa.

*Jareeye* is Somali for a fast horse.
