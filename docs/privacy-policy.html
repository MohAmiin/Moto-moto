Jareeye Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Jareeye is a moto (motorbike) delivery app for Hargeisa. It shows customers the approved moto drivers who are online near them, so they can call a driver and hand over a package. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Jareeye is operated by Mohamed Abdirahman Hassan, Hargeisa, Somaliland ("Jareeye", "we", "us"). You can contact us at jareeye.help@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Everyone who signs up

  • Phone number, used to sign in and as your account name.
  • PIN, which you choose. It is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form; we cannot read it.
  • Name and neighbourhood in Hargeisa.
  • Calls and ratings: when you tap Call on a driver, we record which driver you called and when, and any star rating you give afterwards.

Customers

  • Location, if you allow it, to show the drivers nearest to you. Your position is used while the app is open and is not stored on our servers.

Moto drivers

  • Face photo, bike plate and working area, shown to customers so they know who is coming.
  • ID number and photo of an ID card, if you provide them. Only you and Jareeye staff can see these; they are used to check who you are before approval.
  • Location while you are Online, shared about every 30 seconds so customers nearby can see you on the map. Your position is cleared when you go Offline, and the app does not track you in the background.

How we use it

  • To run the service: sign you in, show online drivers on the map, and let customers call them.
  • To keep it safe: check drivers before approval, and let customers rate drivers.
  • To handle PIN resets: we may call your number to confirm a reset request is really from you.
  • To understand how much the app is used (for example the number of calls per driver).

We do not sell your information, and we do not show advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Customers see an online driver's name, face photo, Jareeye number, plate, rating, approximate distance, map position and phone number.
  • Drivers do not see customers' locations. When a customer calls, the driver sees the customer's phone number, as with any phone call.
  • Jareeye staff can see account details, driver applications and usage, to run and support the service.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores the app's data and photos and handles sign-in (servers in the European Union).
  • Expo delivers app updates.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles are loaded from the internet to draw the map; like any website, the map servers see your device's internet address.
  • Phone calls are normal calls through your mobile operator; Jareeye does not record call audio.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account exists. Driver positions are cleared when the driver goes Offline. Call and rating records are kept to show ratings and usage. When your account is deleted, your profile, photos and application are deleted.

Your choices and deleting your account

  • You can switch off location for Jareeye in your phone's settings at any time; the app then sorts drivers by neighbourhood.
  • Drivers can change their photo and go Offline at any time.
  • To delete your account and data, email jareeye.help@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my Jareeye account" and your phone number. We will confirm by calling that number and delete the account within 30 days.

Children

Jareeye is for people aged 18 and over. Drivers must hold a valid driving licence.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
